Nature of this tool

CanIFlyHere.us is an informational planning resource for drone operators. It queries publicly available FAA geospatial data and presents that information in a simplified format to assist with pre-flight planning. It is not an FAA-approved product, not an official airspace authorization system, and does not replace any regulatory requirement.

No authorization granted

A result of "no restrictions found" from this tool does not constitute flight authorization from the FAA or any other authority. Airspace authorization under FAA rules (including 14 CFR Part 107 and recreational flying rules under 49 U.S.C. § 44809) must be obtained through official FAA channels such as LAANC, DroneZone, or direct coordination with air traffic control. Pilots are solely responsible for obtaining all required authorizations before flight.

Data limitations

This tool queries six FAA data layers but does not represent the complete picture of airspace restrictions that may apply to any given location. Restrictions not covered include:

  • Temporary Flight Restrictions (TFRs) issued for emergencies, VIP movements, wildfires, stadium events, or other purposes
  • Special Use Airspace not covered by the queried layers (some MOAs, restricted areas, warning areas)
  • Local, city, county, or state ordinances restricting drone flight
  • Private property restrictions or trespass laws
  • Facility-specific rules at airports, national parks, or other managed sites

Data displayed is pulled live from FAA servers and may be delayed, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable. Layer updates made by the FAA may not be reflected immediately.
