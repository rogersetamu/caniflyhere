FAA airspace checker for drone pilots

About

CanIFlyHere.us is a free planning resource that overlays publicly available Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) geospatial data onto an interactive map. It helps recreational drone pilots visualize controlled airspace and special use areas before flight.

The tool draws from the FAA UAS Facility Map, national security flight restriction zones (both permanent and part-time), prohibited areas, recreational flyer fixed sites, and FAA Recognized Identification Areas (FRIAs) to give you a quick verdict at any selected point. It does not include temporary flight restrictions (TFRs) or local ordinances.

This application does not grant flight authorization and does not replace your legal compliance requirements. Always consult official FAA sources and local authorities before operating a drone.

Feedback and bug reports are welcome via the project's GitHub repository.
