Privacy Policy

CanIFlyHere.us does not store or transmit personally identifiable information. When you enter coordinates or use the location feature, your position data is processed entirely within your browser and used only to display the map and query FAA geospatial layers. Coordinates are never logged or associated with any user account.

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization through your Google account settings or opt out via your browser's privacy controls.

Continuing to use this site indicates your acceptance of cookies as described above. Questions or concerns can be submitted through the project's GitHub repository.
